# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and inclusive community for everyone who participates in the BT project, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- Communicating with empathy, patience, and respect.
- Giving and accepting constructive technical feedback.
- Focusing disagreement on ideas, evidence, and project impact.
- Respecting privacy, boundaries, and differing levels of experience.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and working to repair harm.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or demeaning language.
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or sexualized content.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where a person is representing the BT project. It also applies to public conduct outside project spaces when that conduct directly affects the safety or integrity of the community.

## Reporting and enforcement

To report conduct in a BT project space, contact a maintainer through a private contact method published on that maintainer's verified GitHub profile. If no suitable private project channel is available, use the platform's abuse-reporting tools or contact [GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/). Do not disclose sensitive details in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities use the separate private process in the [Security Policy](SECURITY.md); that vulnerability channel is not a general conduct-reporting channel.

Project maintainers will review reports in good faith, limit disclosure to people needed for the investigation, and take action appropriate to the context. Possible actions include a private warning, removal of content, temporary participation restrictions, or a permanent ban. Knowingly false or retaliatory reports may also result in corrective action.

Maintainers who have a conflict of interest must not participate in deciding the outcome. Retaliation against reporters, witnesses, or people assisting an investigation is prohibited.
