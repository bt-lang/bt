Bundled SQLite
==============

The sqlite extension enables rusqlite's `bundled` feature. The resulting WASM
module statically contains SQLite. SQLite is in the public domain; the
amalgamation carries the following notice:

The author disclaims copyright to this source code. In place of a legal
notice, here is a blessing:

May you do good and not evil.
May you find forgiveness for yourself and forgive others.
May you share freely, never taking more than you give.

SQLite source and its public-domain statement are available at:
https://www.sqlite.org/copyright.html
